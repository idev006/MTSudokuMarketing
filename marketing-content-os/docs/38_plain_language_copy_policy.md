# Plain-Language Copy Policy

Status: ACTIVE
Date: 2026-08-25
Scope: BiiigBee Marketing Content OS, GPT1 campaign rows, GPT2 handoff, desktop operator workflow, social post packages

## 1. Purpose

All customer-facing copy must be understandable to both:

1. people who already know Sudoku or educational products; and
2. people who do not know Sudoku well but are considering the product for a child, classroom, or practice routine.

The project should not ask customers or operators to understand internal terminology before they can understand the message.

## 2. Document-driven rule

This policy is a project rule. GPT instructions, app labels, templates, captions, headlines, CTA text, text overlays, and post-package reviews should follow it.

If any generated copy is technically correct but difficult for a general parent, teacher, or buyer to understand, it should be revised before publication.

## 3. Required style

Customer copy should use:

- everyday Thai;
- short sentences;
- one idea per sentence when practical;
- concrete benefits;
- clear product identity;
- simple explanation of who the product is for;
- calm, trustworthy educational tone.

## 4. Avoid

Avoid customer-facing copy that relies on:

- internal project terminology;
- validation or SSOT language;
- unexplained acronyms;
- dense marketing jargon;
- dense educational theory;
- English-heavy phrasing when a clear Thai phrase works better;
- unsupported claims;
- technical Sudoku variant names unless the approved product source supports them and the wording explains them simply.

## 5. Good copy pattern

Use this pattern when possible:

```text
This is for who?
What does the child practice?
Why is it useful?
What should the parent/teacher do next?
```

Example safe pattern:

```text
ชุดฝึก 9x9 Sudoku ระดับ EASY สำหรับนักเรียนประถมปลาย
ช่วยให้เด็กเริ่มฝึกคิดเป็นระบบ โดยไม่ต้องเริ่มจากโจทย์ยากเกินไป
มี 500 โจทย์ พร้อมเฉลยครบ ใช้พิมพ์ฝึกได้ตามจังหวะของเด็ก
```

## 6. Review checklist

Before using a post package, check:

- Can a non-expert parent understand the message in one read?
- Is the benefit concrete?
- Is the product truth still safe?
- Are there unsupported guarantees or official claims?
- Is the CTA simple?
- Does the wording sound like a helpful brand, not an internal system?

## 7. GPT1 expectation

GPT1 should generate HOOK, HEADLINE, CAPTION, CTA, and TEXT_OVERLAY in plain language while preserving product truth and controlled vocabulary requirements.

## 8. GPT2 expectation

GPT2 should preserve or improve plain-language clarity in TEMPLATE_HANDOFF and any copy guidance. It must not make the copy more technical merely to sound premium.

## 9. Operator expectation

The desktop app should guide operators with simple labels and direct next actions. Operators should not need to know project internals in order to run the pipeline safely.
