# 06 — Workflow และ Process Engineering

## Production Workflow

```text
Generate
↓
Review
↓
Approve
↓
Generate Image
↓
Visual Review
↓
Schedule
↓
Publish
↓
Measure
↓
Learn
↓
Next Campaign
```

- `COPY_APPROVED`

- DRAFT
- REVIEW
- APPROVED
- IMAGE_READY
- SCHEDULED
- PUBLISHED

## Process Principles

- repeatable
- auditable
- scalable
- low variation
- human approval
- version controlled
- measurable
