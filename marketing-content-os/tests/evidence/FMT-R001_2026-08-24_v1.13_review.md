`FMT-R001` remains **FAIL** on v1.13.

## Impact

- GPT #1 remains Candidate / Not Production.
- GPT #2 candidate activation remains blocked if the gate requires clean raw GPT output.

## Recommended next action

Move `OUTPUT-FMT-001` handling from instruction-only mitigation to deterministic post-processing / cleanup tooling, or explicitly downgrade the raw empty-fence behavior to a known GPT-output limitation when a deterministic extractor can safely remove it without changing TSV row content.
