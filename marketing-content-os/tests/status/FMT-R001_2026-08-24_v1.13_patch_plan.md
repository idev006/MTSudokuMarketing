## v1.13 Patch Intent

- every code fence in the entire response must be tagged `tsv`;
- every code fence must contain the canonical `ROW_ID` header and at least one data row;
- untagged fences are forbidden;
- empty fences are forbidden;
- adjacent fences are forbidden;
- placeholder fences are forbidden;
- no standalone fence may appear before the TSV block;
- `Part 1 of 1` must stay plain text outside the fence;
- final scan must remove any bad fence before the answer is sent.
